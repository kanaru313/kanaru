# System architecture: lyrics

Part of [System architecture](system-architecture.md).

### Lyrics

Lyrics are read when a track plays, for the playing track only, and not stored: no column, no slower scan, and a `.lrc` edited outside the app shows at the next play. Translations are stored (schema 8): each translated source line with its translation, in `lyric_translations` (see [section 7](system-architecture-database.md#7-library-database-schema)). Online lyrics results, the per-song choice and pick, and rejected matches have tables too (schema 9; the choice and the rejected text hash came in schema 10, same section); the provider contract, the resolution order and the status are below the table. The order depends on the song's choice: the order is fixed: the song's pick, then local (tags or `.lrc`), then cache > network; `local` puts local first, and `off` turns the lyrics off (a remembered `local` comes back on turning them on) (see "Resolution and status").

Online lyrics (provider contract): `core/lyrics/online/` holds a pure layer. A `LyricsProvider` (`id`, `displayName`, `hosts`, `lookup`, `search`, `fetch`) is stateless and async and does no I/O except through the `LyricsHttp` it is given. The host copies its id, name and hosts once into a `LyricsProviderRegistration` (`registerLyricsProviders`, `lyricsProvidersProvider`); later changes of the provider's getters are ignored. Ids must be unique and each host a public DNS name: no IP literal (so no loopback, link-local or private address), single-label or `.localhost`/`.local`/`.internal`/`.lan`/`.home.arpa`/`.localdomain` name, port or path. `LyricsHttp` is the only network capability: `get` (GET) and `send` (GET or POST for plugin sources; see below), host in the snapshot, `https`, no port or user info (else `blockedHost` before any socket), 10 s connect and 20 s per send, 30 s for the whole call with its busy waits (then `network`, or `rateLimited` with the `Retry-After` of the last 429 or 503 seen), 4 MiB body cap for lyrics (translation: 2 MiB), no redirects (it reuses `sendTranslationRequest`, so a sent request cannot be cancelled), and the app's `User-Agent: Kanaru/<version> (+repository)` is always sent by `get` (besides it, `get` sends only `accept: application/json`; `send` forwards the source's allowed headers, with `accept` defaulting to `*/*`); the test-only `LyricsHttp.forTesting` allows `http`, a port and loopback. A 429 or 503 asking for at most 5 s (a 503 without `Retry-After` asks 1 s; a 429 without it is final) is waited out inside `LyricsHttp.get` and sent again, at most twice and one request at a time (at most 10 s added); the last answer is returned as is, so the provider contract is unchanged. `send(LyricsHttpRequest)` (`lyrics_http_request.dart`) shares one loop with `get` (`lyrics_http_send_loop.dart`: abandon, then consent, before every send; retries; the overall timeout; consent withdrawn during the timeout is `notConsented`) and differs in: method `GET` or `POST` with a body (at most 256 KiB, POST only, every byte 0..255) sent as bytes; the source's headers lower-cased, with `host`, `accept-encoding` (the client keeps its own gzip handling), `connection`, `content-length`, `transfer-encoding`, `te`, `upgrade`, `keep-alive`, `proxy-*`, `trailer` and `expect` dropped silently, the source's `user-agent` sent when it gives a non-blank one (else the app's `Kanaru/<version> (+repository)`), `accept` defaulting to `*/*` and any other value kept; the answer as raw bytes (at most 4 MiB, no UTF-8 check; charset is decoded by the caller) with status, headers (lower-case, repeated values joined by `, `), url and `sends` (requests made, retries included); and a `deadline` (time the caller has left) that shortens the 30 s overall timeout to the smaller of the two, with no request at all when none is left, and a socket still open at the deadline is closed. `send` takes an `onSend` callback called before each actual send so the caller counts sends even when the call fails. A bad method, header name or value (CR, LF or other control or non-ASCII characters, a name that is not a token) or body throws `LyricsRequestRejected(reason)`, which is not a `LyricsFetchKind` and sends nothing; an over-cap response is still `LyricsFetchException(invalidResponse)`. Both calls go through `sendTranslationRequest`, which takes an optional `bodyBytes` and `decode: false` (raw bytes in `TranslationResponse.bytes`) without changing its translation callers. `abandoned` is asked before every send, the first and each retry included (so an abandoned lookup puts nothing more on the wire); that is the only cancellable point: when every caller of the in-flight lookup has abandoned it by then (an automatic lookup provider is disposed on a song change or when Now Playing closes; a manual lookup never abandons, and a caller that joins the same key's lookup, such as Retry or the song coming back, keeps it going), `LyricsRequestAbandoned` ends the lookup with no failure and no backoff. The automatic backoff (60 s minimum) starts only after those retries fail, and a `rateLimited` failure shows "{provider} is busy. Try again in a moment." instead of "Couldn't reach {provider}". Failures are `LyricsFetchException(kind)` with `retryAfter` (whole seconds clamped to 1 s..15 min; an HTTP date gives none). The host checks the output of every provider's `lookup`, `search` and `fetch` (`lyrics_candidate_checks.dart`) before ranking or storing, in `runOnlineLyricsLookup` and in the manual search. A candidate needs a `remoteId` of 1..256 characters without control characters, and a title and artist that are non-empty after trimming and at most 512 characters; an over-long album and a duration outside 0..24 h are dropped; the `providerId` is forced to the registration id. Lyrics texts lose control characters except `\n` and `\t`, `\r\n` becomes `\n`, a text over 1 MiB in UTF-8 is dropped, and synced text that `parseLyricsText` does not read as timed lyrics moves to plain (or is dropped when plain text exists or nothing is left to show). A list keeps at most 20 results, the first of each `remoteId`, and drops an invalid candidate without dropping the list; a non-empty list with no valid candidate is `LyricsFetchException(invalidResponse)`, so a broken source is never cached as "no lyrics". Matching (`lyrics_match.dart`, keys in `lyrics_match_keys.dart`): a candidate passes when the duration differs by at most 3 s, the title is equal, joined-equal or at least 0.85 similar (normalized Levenshtein over code points; `sortKey` fold, `&` as `and`, noise such as `feat.`, `Official Video`, `Remaster`, `Radio Edit` stripped from brackets and ` - ` suffixes, live/remix/acoustic kept), and the artist matches (`lyrics_artist_match.dart`; any artist: the full artist strings are equal, as in `Earth, Wind & Fire`, or any name of the library tag equals or is at least 0.85 similar to any name of the remote string, so `Ngân Ngân, Châu Khải Phong` matches a record by `Chau Khai Phong` whatever the order; the remote string is split on `,` and ` & ` first and then each part on ` x `; a name of one or two code points after folding must be exactly equal; placeholder names such as `Unknown Artist`, `Various Artists`, `Various`, `Nhiều ca sĩ` and `VA` never match), and it has lyrics or is instrumental. The gate has two steps because a two-step source (a plugin) answers its search without lyrics: a record with no body at all passes on its metadata (`LyricsMatch.metadataGood`), its lyrics are fetched with `fetch`, and the fetched text goes through the body checks and the reject-by-text check below before it can be chosen. A record that came with a body (a one-step source) is judged as before, and in the ranking a record without a body comes after every good record with one. A candidate whose record is rejected, or whose text hashes like a rejected record (`candidateContentHash`, the same `lyricsContentHash` as translations), never matches. `isAutoSearchable` needs a title, a real first artist and a duration. The cache key is sha256 of `v1.k<textKeysVersion>|artist|title|album|seconds`; a song with no title and an artist that is missing or a placeholder such as `Unknown Artist`, `Various Artists`, `VA` or `Nhiều ca sĩ` also appends its track key (path, else uri) as a scope (`LyricsTarget.cacheKey`), so two such songs never share a row.

Consent and the `.lrc` writer: `OnlineLyricsHttpFactory` (`online_lyrics_http_factory.dart`, `onlineLyricsHttpFactoryProvider`) is the only source of a `LyricsHttp`. `forManual(registration)` needs `onlineLyrics.manualConsent`, `forAuto(registration)` the separate `onlineLyrics.autoConsent` (manual consent never implies auto). Consent is global: one answer for every enabled source, not tied to a source or its hosts (the host allowlist in `LyricsHttp` stays, as the security boundary); otherwise it throws `LyricsFetchException(notConsented)` before any socket, so Retry, the dialog and automatic lookup all go through the same check. Consent is read before every send (the first, each retry and a lookup's second request), so withdrawing it stops the next request at once (a request already sent runs to completion, but an answer that arrives after withdrawal is dropped and nothing is stored). A withdrawal during the overall 30 s timeout also ends as `notConsented`, not as a timeout, so it starts no backoff. Settings > Playback > Lyrics (`lyrics_setting_rows.dart`) has a row "Online lyrics search permission" (manual, manual and automatic, or not allowed) with a confirmed Withdraw button that calls `withdrawOnlineLyricsConsent()` (both flags and `autoFetch` off in one write); saved lyrics stay, and the next search or the automatic switch shows the notice again. The shared `HttpClient` (`lyricsHttpClientProvider`, 10 s connect timeout, closed with the container) is created only after a request is allowed. `confirmOnlineLyricsNotice(navigator, container, auto:)` (`lib/shared/online_lyrics_notice_dialog.dart`) asks once for all sources and takes no `WidgetRef`: the settings notifier is read from the container before the dialog opens, so the caller may unmount; Continue writes `acknowledgeOnlineLyricsNotice(auto:)` (manual: `manualConsent`; auto: both flags and `autoFetch` in one write); `enableAutoOnlineLyrics()` turns the switch on again when the consent is already kept. `LrcSidecarWriter` (`lrc_write_result.dart` (`LrcWriteResult`, `LrcSidecarWriter`), `lrc_sidecar_writer.dart`, `lrc_sidecar_write_steps.dart`, `lrcSidecarWriterProvider`) is called only by Save lyrics (`OnlineLyricsActions.save` for online lyrics, `saveLocalAsLrc` for lyrics from the song's tags): a pick or an automatic match writes no file. `write(audioPath, body, {replace})` never throws and never deletes. Text: the first of the synced and plain lyrics that `parseLyricsText` accepts (`storedLyricsText`, the rule `parseStoredLyrics` uses, so the file holds what was on screen), `\r\n` to `\n`, one final newline, UTF-8 without BOM, at most 1 MiB; none for instrumental or unparsable text (`nothingToWrite`). Order: the song must be a regular file and both it and its folder, symbolic links resolved, inside a library root and outside every excluded folder (`outsideLibrary`); every existing `<name>.lrc` / `<name>.LRC` is classified without following links; without `replace` an existing entry gives `identical` when the first one holds exactly the new text (read through `readM3uBytes` with the 1 MiB cap, so a FIFO, folder or oversized file is never identical and never blocks) and `exists` otherwise; an entry that is neither a regular file nor a link (a folder, FIFO, socket) gives `trashFailed` before anything moves, also without `replace`, so the user is never asked to confirm a replace that cannot work; stale `.kanaru-lrc-*` folders next to the song older than 10 minutes are removed (best effort, links never followed); the text is written to `lyrics.lrc` inside a private `createTemp('.kanaru-lrc-')` folder (a full disk fails here, before anything is trashed); each classified old file is re-checked and moved to the trash (`.lrc`, then `.LRC`); both names are checked absent again; `rename`. The temp folder is always removed, so no placeholder or partial file is ever visible. A refusal of the trash for the first old file is `trashFailed` (nothing changed); a timeout of the trash for the first old file is `trashTimedOut` (the isolate may still have moved it, so the old file may be in the trash and no new file was written); any failure after a file reached the trash (second trash, a file that reappeared, the rename) is `trashedNotWritten`. A file system error logs only the OS error code and gives `failed`. A gap of well under a millisecond remains between the last check and the rename, where a file made by another program would be replaced. `FileTrash` (`core/native/file_trash.dart`, `fileTrashProvider`) moves a file with GLib's `g_file_trash` through `dart:ffi` inside `Isolate.run`, the caller waiting at most 10 s (`timedOut`; the isolate may still finish). The symbols come from the running process (the Linux runner links GLib), else `libgio-2.0.so.0`; a load failure is `unsupported`. Only the three leading fields of `GError` are read (domain compared with `g_io_error_quark()`, code `NOT_FOUND` gives `notFound`, `NOT_SUPPORTED` gives `unsupported`, anything else `failed`), the error, the `GFile` and the C strings are always freed, and only the code is logged, never a path or message. `OnlineLyricsActions.save(target, origin, {replace})` (`online_lyrics_save_action.dart`) takes the stored text of the record by remote id (pin, else cache), writes it and, on `written` or `identical`, invalidates `playingLocalLyricsProvider` and reads the song's own lyrics again with the current `preferEmbeddedLyrics`: when the result is the `.lrc` with the same content hash nothing is forgotten: the pick and the choice stay, so a pinned online version keeps showing and the file is the song's local lyrics from now on; otherwise the outcome says why the saved file is not what shows (`tagPreferred` when tags won, `otherFile` for a different file such as another `.LRC` spelling). A missing text gives `result: null`. A save is guarded per song: `isSaving(trackKey)` is true while one runs, and a second `save` of the same song is ignored (`result: null`); a save refused for that reason shows no message. Lyrics from the tags are saved by `saveLocalLyricsAsLrc` (`core/lyrics/local_lyrics_save_action.dart`): it reads the tags again, builds the `Lyrics` the way the loader does (`lyricsFromTags`: `SYLT` lines win over the text, as on screen) and writes synced lyrics as `[mm:ss.fff]` lines (`formatLrcLines`, `lrc_format.dart`; line breaks inside a line become a space) and plain lyrics as they are, through the same writer (library check, trash, identical detection). It changes no pick or choice; `kept: tagPreferred` when "prefer embedded lyrics" is on. The UI (`features/player/lyrics/lyrics_save_flow.dart`, `runLyricsSaveTargets`) runs after the "Save lyrics to" child modal (`LyricsSaveTargetsModal`) has closed the whole modal stack, so the snackbar shows on Now Playing; it saves to every chosen target (only `.lrc` exists), confirms replacing by naming every existing file that goes to the trash (`a.lrc and a.LRC` when both exist; found without blocking the UI isolate by `existingLrcSidecars`) and the song's title; it says only that the old file is moved to the trash, on every disk, since GLib picks the trash by device, and tells every result with its own snackbar, partial ones included (a `exists` that comes back after a confirmed replace says another lyrics file just appeared; no record on screen says there are no lyrics to save). It acts on the `LyricsActionRunner` snapshot (`lyrics_action_runner.dart`: container, root navigator, messenger, song and lyrics source, taken once when a menu opens), so a track change during a dialog still saves the song the user saw; the runner logs only the error type.

Manual search: the Find lyrics online… row (`FindLyricsChoice`) on the Advanced page of the song modal (`song_modal_advanced_page.dart`; shown for any local file, tagged or not, when the library database is open) and in the queue row menu (`trackLinkMenuItems(showFindLyrics:)`, for a local `Track`) is carried out by `TrackMenuRunner` or `QueueRowMenuTarget`, which capture the `ProviderContainer`, the root `NavigatorState` and the `ScaffoldMessengerState` when the menu opens, so a row rebuilt away before the pick changes nothing. `showLyricsPicker` (`lib/shared/lyrics_picker/`) asks the consent notice (Cancel: no dialog, no request), re-reads a library song by path (one cache key for every entry point), opens the Choose lyrics dialog as a wide layered modal (`showLayeredModal(wide: true)`) under `UncontrolledProviderScope`: from the lyrics modal's Find row it stacks over that modal (`parentModalContext`), which stays open underneath, and its back arrow, Esc or scrim close only the picker; from the track menu it is a top-level modal with no back arrow. A pick closes the whole stack (`closeLayeredModalStack`) and goes to `OnlineLyricsActions.pick`: the pin in the database, no file, then a snackbar. The source filter is one chip left of the query field (`LyricsPickerSearchRow`, `LyricsPickerSourceChip`) that opens the "Search sources" child modal (`showLyricsPickerSourcesModal`): the filter rows and the off sources, each with its own Search; `lyricsResultSources` and `effectiveSourceFilter` give the dialog and the pane the same sources. The dialog searches on open only for a song with a real title; the prefill leaves out a placeholder artist ("Unknown Artist"), so an artist-only song gets its artist in the field and waits for Search. A song without a real title or artist opens the dialog with an empty query: the results pane shows a note ("Type the artist and title, then press Search") instead of a spinner, Search stays off while the field is blank, and nothing is sent until the user searches (the file name is never prefilled or sent); its cache key is scoped to the track, so rejects stay per song. The pane words a failed search with `lyricsFailureMessage`. The preview shows the start time of each synced line (`mm:ss.xx`, `h:mm:ss.xx` from one hour) in a fixed-width monospace column, with a wrapped line staying under the text; an empty synced line shows its time and a dim note, and plain lyrics show text only. The search state is `lyricsSearchControllerProvider(target)` (per-provider state, ranking, an older search's late results dropped). A search asks the enabled sources only; a disabled source is asked only by an explicit `searchDisabled({ids})` call from the picker, for that search (`LyricsSearchState.onDemand`), and a new search drops it again. Settings never change, automatic lookups never ask a disabled source, and the dialog builds provider names and footer hosts, and the body fetch finds its source, from the enabled sources plus the on-demand ones (`searchRegistrations`), never an unsearched disabled source. A failed on-demand source retries alone (`retrySource`, which asks it again through `searchDisabled`); the lyrics modal's Find row opens the picker also when every source is off. A hand-selected result stays selected and listed when on-demand answers re-rank the list. A pick from a disabled source writes the pin only.

Resolution and status (`playing_lyrics_status_provider.dart`, `playing_lyrics_status.dart`, `online_lyrics_resolution.dart`, `lyrics_choice.dart`, `lyrics_source_resolution.dart`, `playing_lyrics_resolution_provider.dart`, `playing_lyrics_source_provider.dart`, `auto_lyrics_lookup_provider.dart`): `playingLyricsStatusProvider` turns the playing track into one `PlayingLyricsStatus` (`null` when nothing plays): `LyricsReading`, `LyricsSearching(providerId)`, `LyricsFound(lyrics, origin?)` or `LyricsNone(reason, origin?)` with reason `noLyrics`, `notFound`, `error`, `off`, `paused` (with `resumeAt`, the end of the automatic pause) or `instrumental`; `OnlineLyricsOrigin` holds provider id, remote id, whether the user pinned it and the length difference. The only statuses a consumer sees are these; nothing else (retry times, request details) is exposed. The song's choice (`LyricsChoice`: `local`, `online`, `off`; a null or unknown stored mode reads as no choice, `null`; `off:local` reads as `off` and remembers `local`) sets the order. `resolveChoice` (pure, in `online_lyrics_resolution.dart`) is the one place it is written: `off` gives `off` and watches no lookup; a usable pick wins unless `local` is chosen (this includes a legacy row with no mode and a pick); otherwise local lyrics (tags or `.lrc`) win when there are any, else a local file takes the cached answer (found text, instrumental, or a `not_found` younger than 14 days), then an automatic lookup, and other songs get no lyrics. The effective choice is `local`, `online` (a pick or an automatic match) or `null` when nothing applies; an automatic match is told apart by `origin.pinned == false`, and the label then reads "Auto · X". Two lists of sources: `lyricsProvidersProvider` holds the enabled sources in the user's order, which are the ones that run; `lyricsSourceNamesProvider` (`lyrics_source_names.dart`) maps every known provider id to a `LyricsSourceName` (`displayName`, `author?`, `removed`), which is what names a stored row. Both come from the installed lyrics plugins only: with none enabled `lyricsProvidersProvider` is empty, nothing is looked up or searched, and the automatic lookup switch in settings is disabled (`settingsAutoOnlineLyricsNoSource`); the stored `autoFetch` and `autoConsent` are left as they are, so they apply again when a plugin is turned on. The automatic lookup (`auto_lyrics_lookup_provider.dart`, `playing_lyrics_resolution_provider.dart`) needs at least one enabled plugin. The cache stream (`onlineLyricsCacheProvider`) ignores rows of unknown ids, lists enabled sources first in the user's order, then the other known sources by name, and keeps a row of a known source that is not enabled only when it found lyrics. `lyricsSourcesLoadedProvider` is true once the list is complete: until then the cache stream is loading, a song that needs the cache reads as `LyricsReading` (local lyrics, picks and off still show), and lookups and manual searches wait, so a source still loading is never mistaken for a missing one and nothing is sent on a cold start. A song is "not found" only when every enabled source has a recent `not_found` row (or a found row with no usable text); a miss of a source that is not enabled never counts. An automatic lookup asks only the enabled sources with no row or a stale `not_found`; a manual one asks every enabled source. A fresh failure of one source shows over another source's miss. Resolving never writes. Without a library database, or when the pin, fields or cache cannot be read (logged once), only local lyrics are used and nothing is requested. While the pin, the fresh tag fields or the local read load, the status is `LyricsReading`, or the previous status of the same song so views do not flash. The target (`LyricsTarget`) takes a library song's fields from its database row, not from the queue's older `Track`; a title equal to the file name is no title. Automatic lookups run only while something watches the status (Now Playing is shown), and only when `autoFetch` is on, the automatic consent is accepted, the target is auto-searchable and the session is not in backoff. The watching resolution holds `autoLyricsLookupProvider(target)` (`auto_lyrics_lookup_provider.dart`), which waits the debounce (`onlineLyricsDebounceProvider`, 1 s) before asking the keep-alive `OnlineLyricsLookups` to start: a song skipped within the debounce disposes it and nothing is sent, so ten quick skips send one lookup. A lookup takes `lookup`'s record when it passes the gate; a good match that came without lyrics is fetched in rank order, at most 3 `fetch` calls per lookup, and the first with lyrics (or instrumental) whose text is not a duplicate of a rejected one wins (none: a `not_found` row; a failing `fetch` throws and writes nothing; a one-step source sends no extra request); a plain-only record (no synced text, not instrumental) also runs `search`, and the best good match replaces it only when that match has synced text (two requests for a one-step source, three when the best match has to be fetched; a match without a body counts only after its fetch). If that extra search or fetch fails with an error other than withdrawn consent, the plain record is kept (no failure, no backoff); withdrawn consent still ends the lookup. Rejected records, and records whose text hashes like a rejected one, are excluded from the gate and the ranking. Lookups are deduped by cache key, run to completion even if the song changed (a sent request cannot be aborted), write the cache only for whitelisted answers, and a failure sets the in-memory backoff (60 s, or the `Retry-After`, up to 15 min, plus up to 30 % jitter) and no row. The status does not start a lookup from its own cache write: the lookup stays watched until the cache answers. A lookup that ends with nothing recorded (Settings Clear dropped its failure while it ran) restarts the provider after the same debounce, so the song never stays on searching. A song with no local lyrics and no usable cache entry takes its state from the lookup, in this order: running (searching); an automatic lookup due (searching, so an old failure is not shown while a new one is about to run); failed (error); paused with an automatic lookup wanted (`paused`, with `resumeAt`; the pause's end is watched, so an extended pause updates it); otherwise no lyrics. `playingLyricsResolutionProvider` (`playing_lyrics_resolution_provider.dart`, autoDispose) wires the inputs of `resolveChoice` and keeps the whole previous `LyricsSourceResolution` record of the same song (`lyrics_source_resolution.dart`) while the song's own lyrics are read again; `playingLyricsStatusProvider` is a select of its status. `playingLyricsSourceProvider` (`playing_lyrics_source_provider.dart`, autoDispose) gives `chosen`, `effective`, `pin`, `hasLocal` and the status from that same resolution, so no extra database stream is opened per song. `OnlineLyricsLookups.forgetFailures()` drops every remembered failure, and a lookup already running then records no failure and starts no pause; the Settings "Clear" button calls it after a successful clear, together with the backoff's `clear()`. `OnlineLyricsActions` (`online_lyrics_actions.dart`, `onlineLyricsActionsProvider`, usable from a `ProviderContainer`): `retry` clears the backoff and looks up once; `choose` sets the choice (`online` stores it when a pick exists, else removes the pin row; `local` stores it and keeps a stored pick; `off` keeps a stored pick and stores `off:local` when the choice was `local`, else `off`; choosing the mode already stored writes nothing; after `online` or `local`, changed or not, the song's own lyrics are read again); `turnOn` leaves `off`: `off:local` back to `local`, else the pick as `online`, else the row is removed; `save` writes the stored lyrics of a record as the song's `.lrc` (see the writer above); `keep` makes an automatic match the pick by its remote id, and does nothing when its text is no longer cached; `reject` (`online_lyrics_reject_action.dart`) remembers the match by id and by the content hash of the lyrics on screen, drops the cached rows with that text and the pick when it is that match (a `local`, `off` or `off:local` choice stays), and sends nothing: the fixed order runs again; `pick` pins a candidate with its text in the database and writes no file. All requests go through the consent-checked lookups. Rejected matches (and records whose text duplicates one) are hidden only from automatic lookups: the Choose lyrics dialog (`rankCandidates(keepRejected: true)`) lists them last, dimmed and labeled "Rejected", never selects one by itself (the first result that has lyrics or is a good match is selected by itself, and when its fetched text is a rejected one the next such result is selected instead, and so it is when the fetch of an automatic pick finds no lyrics; a result the user clicked stays selected, and so does one whose fetch failed, with its error; at most 3 automatic fetches follow one search, then the selection stays; a result that came without lyrics is selectable, selecting it fetches the lyrics through `LyricsSearchController`, one fetch at a time per result, with a progress indicator in the preview and the usual failure sentence on error; "Use these lyrics" stays off until they are there; a newer search makes the older search and fetches send nothing more, `OnlineLyricsHttpFactory.forManual(abandoned:)`), and picking one pins it and deletes its reject row in the same transaction (`pickOnlineLyrics`). Streams parse stored text once and drop unchanged rows, so the same `Lyrics` instance reaches `LyricsView` across unrelated database writes. The "Saved lyrics" row of Settings > Data & storage > Caches reads `watchOnlineLyricsStats` and lists only non-zero parts: songs with lyrics (found rows plus picks), their size, songs not found (any stored row that is not a found answer, unknown statuses included), source choices (every pin without a pick, whatever its mode, so a pin is counted once and Clear stays on while any row is stored) and rejected versions; "Nothing saved" when all are zero. Clear deletes all three tables and never touches `.lrc` files. What leaves the machine: title, primary artist (`/api/get`; `/api/search` only for a one-artist tag), rounded duration (`/api/get` only) and, in the manual search, the typed text, plus the `User-Agent`; never paths, file names or device information. Auto lookup with the setting off, without the automatic consent, for a song with local lyrics, without a library, or while Now Playing is closed sends nothing.

Lyrics of any library song, without playing it: `trackLyricsPreviewProvider(path)` (`core/lyrics/track_lyrics_preview_provider.dart`, auto-dispose family; the local read is `trackLocalLyricsProvider` in `track_local_lyrics_provider.dart`) gives an `AsyncValue<TrackLyricsPreview?>` (the `Lyrics` and its kind: `.lrc`, embedded or online with the source id) for the Song info lyrics card and its full lyrics page. It is cache only: the cache step is `statusFromCache` over `onlineLyricsCacheProvider` and a miss is no lyrics. It never watches `autoLyricsLookupProvider` or any lookup provider, so opening a song's details sends no request; the cost is that a song whose answer is not saved yet (or is being looked up as the playing song) shows no lyrics until it is cached. Choice `off` and a song outside the library resolve to none or local only. The `playing_*` providers are not used or changed. Find online on the card closes the modal and runs `FindLyricsChoice` through the usual runner.

Source naming: there is no credit row under the lyrics. The lyrics button names where they come from in its tooltip ("Lyrics from <source> · Synced", `lyricsSourceTooltipTitle` in `features/player/lyrics/source/lyrics_source_labels.dart`) and by its icon (the plugin's icon for online lyrics). Its height is 85 % of the window up to 720 px with a 440 px floor, below which it scrolls (`lyricsPickerContentHeight`); the result list returns to the top on every search and source filter change.

#### Translation

`lyricsTranslationProvider(appLang)` (`core/lyrics_translation/lyrics_translation_controller.dart`, auto-dispose family; the argument is the app UI language, the default target) turns the playing lyrics, the `lyricsTranslation` settings, the per-song target, and the saved rows into one `LyricsTranslationState`: `Unavailable` (no lyrics for the playing uri, or no library database), `Off` (carries the lyrics hash and target for the modal), `NeedsNotice`, `Loading`, `Ready` (one entry per lyrics line; `fromOtherService` is true when displaying a saved row from another translate plugin), `SameLanguage` (`fromOtherService`, `refreshing`, `retranslateFailure` like `Ready`), `Failed(kind)`. Only a Now Playing widget that watches it translates: closing Now Playing disposes it, Cover only does not watch it.

Rendering: `LyricsView` watches the state and the display setting and passes `(translations, pending, display, direction)` to `SyncedLyricsView` or `PlainLyricsView`; `TranslatedLineText` (`features/player/lyrics/translation/`) draws a line once for both. Under each line the translation is 0.62x the line size, Replace makes it the main line over the original at 0.5x. The added text has one size and weight in both states and changes only color with the current line, so it never changes height when the current line moves (the main line keeps its own bold switch). `Loading` puts a `LyricsTranslationPlaceholder` bar under every non-blank line; one controller in `LyricsView` pulses all bars through `FadeTransition` in a `RepaintBoundary` each, and it runs only while loading and when animations are allowed. `SyncedLyricsView` puts the current line back at the anchor without a glide when `translations`, `display` or `pending` really change (`listEquals`), unless the user scrolled a moment ago; scrolling code is in the `SyncedLyricsScroll` mixin. `Failed`, `Ready(retranslateFailure)` and `SameLanguage(retranslateFailure)` show `LyricsTranslationBanner` at the top (reason, "Open settings" for key failures, and Retry except for key, too-long and unsupported-language failures). The lyrics carry no credit: it is on the header translate button. Arabic, Hebrew and Persian translations are set right to left.

- **Service ids and the effective service**: a service is a `TranslationServiceId` (`translation_service.dart`, an extension type over `String`): a translate plugin's install id; the stored service can be `null` (default). `effectiveTranslationServiceProvider` (`translation_service_resolution.dart`) turns the stored service into the one a request goes to: a plugin id is itself while its translate record is live, and `null` once the records were read and it is not there; `null` (nothing is sent) while the records load, when reading them failed, when there is no library database, or when no service is selected. `needsNoticeFor(prefs, id)` is the one consent rule, used by the controller, `TranslationJobs.run`, the modal, the `T` key and the plugin runs. `translationServiceSweepProvider` (listened from `main.dart`) writes the cleanup once the records are really read from the database: a stored plugin service that is gone becomes `null`, `show` becomes `false`, and the gone ids leave `noticeAcknowledged` in one settings write; a plugin installed again asks the notice again. Consent is per plugin id: an update that adds hosts does not ask again.
- **Service of a song (newest explicit choice wins)**: the service a song translates with is `TranslationChoice` (`translation_scope.dart`, `resolveTranslationChoice`, pure), made from the default (`prefs.service`, stamped by `TranslationPrefs.allChosenAt`), the song's own choice (`lyric_translation_prefs` row: `service`, `tune_json`, `chosen_at`, read through `songTranslationOverridesProvider`, a stream of every row by lyrics hash, kept running by a listener in `TranslationJobs.build` only while jobs are listened to (the controller watches them)) and the queue's (`queueTranslationChoiceProvider`, below). Stamps are epoch ms from `translationClockProvider`. The song's choice wins only when strictly newer than `allChosenAt` (null counts as oldest) and than the queue's; the queue's wins when strictly newer than `allChosenAt`; so on a tie the broader tier wins; a tier whose plugin is not installed is absent; a losing choice is never deleted, so a later newer narrow choice wins again. Only "All songs" in the service picker of the translation modal sets `allChosenAt`; **Settings never stamps it**, so changing the default in Settings never beats a song's choice. `translationChoiceFor(get, hash)` (`translation_scope_providers.dart`) is the one place that gathers the inputs: the controller calls it with `ref.watch` (selecting only the song's row, so another song's change does not rebuild it) and jobs with `ref.read` (`readTranslationChoiceFor`); `null` means not known yet (records or song choices still loading), which the controller shows as `Loading` (a failed records read stays `Unavailable`). The choice is in `TranslationTargetInfo.choice` (part of its equality), and every place that names "the song's service" reads it (service row, action bar, status line, banner, retranslate snackbar, preview and switch); `effectiveTranslationServiceProvider` keeps meaning "the default", for Settings and the sweep. The saved-translation cache is still read first: a song whose choice lost to a newer "All songs" still shows the version saved by its own plugin, with the "Translate with <default>" bar, and nothing is requested. `TranslationJobs.run` and each plugin chunk compare the key's service with the resolved one, so a newer broader choice made while a long job waits stops the next chunk. A system clock set back makes a new choice look older than an earlier one (accepted).
- **Queue tier** (`core/lyrics_translation/queue_translation_choice.dart`): `queueTranslationChoiceProvider` is a keep-alive notifier of `ScopedTranslationChoice?` (service, tune, `chosenAt`) for the queue playing now. The app shell keeps it alive (`ref.listen` next to the queue history recorder), because the controller lives only while Now Playing is open and a notifier built lazily there would miss every queue edit made before. A queue is identified by `queueFingerprint`: the SHA-256 of the track URIs in the order the queue was opened (`unshuffledOrder ?? order`, not play order), so, for a queue not edited since it was opened, tapping another song of the same album, shuffling, a new shuffled round of repeat-all-shuffled, and even opening the same tracks shuffled keep it; `hashCode` is not used because it is not stable across runs. The choice **ends** when the player announces on `PlayerService.replacedQueues` a replaced queue whose fingerprint differs from the queue now playing (opening other tracks, clearing the queue down to other tracks), and when the queue becomes empty (removing the last song announces nothing, so the notifier also listens to the queue lists). Adding, removing, moving tracks and play next keep it. The notifier follows the stream like the queue history does: a finished stream means the player rebuilt, so it subscribes to the new one. Known limits: *Clear queue* on a queue that holds only the playing song keeps the choice, since the fingerprint is the same as reopening that song; and after the queue was edited (add, play next, remove, move), reopening the same album or list from the library is a different queue (its fingerprint no longer matches), so the choice ends. The choice is **saved with the playback session** in the session extra `translationQueue` = `{service, tune, chosenAt, queueKey}` through `PlayerService.setSessionExtra`, so it survives a restart; `queueKey` is the fingerprint of the queue at the last write, recomputed only when the queue's lists change identity and written only when the hash changed. At start a value of the wrong shape, or whose `queueKey` is not the fingerprint of the restored queue, is dropped and removed from the session. While the saver is frozen after a restore (see the playback doc), a choice made then reaches the disk only once it unfreezes. A restored choice still goes through the network notice gate like any other.
- **Order of checks** (`lyrics_translation_derive.dart`): show off or no service selected (`Off`), then the song's resolved service (unknown: `Loading`, or `Unavailable` when the records could not be read; no service: `Off`, e.g. when a stored plugin is gone), then the notice gate (`noticeAcknowledged` must hold the effective service; nothing is read or sent before), then the per-song target stream and the saved rows (each still loading is `Loading`, no request), then a running job (the row on screen, the selected service's or else another's, stays as `Ready(refreshing: true)` or `SameLanguage(refreshing: true)`; `Loading` only when there is none), the selected service's row, another service's row: first a row with translated lines (a translate plugin that still has a record, removed ones included), else a row that says the lyrics are already in the target language, only from a plugin still installed (a removed plugin's verdict is not trusted); it shows `SameLanguage(fromOtherService)` and makes no request, and the selected service's own row, when it exists, always wins. A remembered failure is then `Ready(retranslateFailure)` (or `SameLanguage(retranslateFailure)`) on the row shown; `Failed` only when there is no row to show. Only when a request is needed does the controller arm a 1 s timer (`translationDebounceProvider`); it is the only thing tied to the controller's disposal, because Riverpod runs `onDispose` on every rebuild.
- **Jobs** (`translation_jobs.dart`, keep-alive): keyed by (lyrics hash, target, service); one at a time per key, never cancelled in normal use once a chunk is sent (a plugin chunk still waiting is dropped when its song is no longer current), so a started job finishes and saves its own row even if the song changed (the view only reads the current hash). `run` refuses without a request when the service's notice is not accepted, so Retranslate, Retry and a timer cannot bypass the gate. Lines go out in chunks of the translator's `chunkLimits` (a plugin's `translate` block, capped by the job limits, and at most 20 chunks per job, else `tooLong`); finished chunks are kept for the session, so Retry after a failure sends only the rest, unless the plugin was updated (its `updatedAt` changed) or its config was saved (`clearFailures`). A result is saved only after every chunk passed, under `provider` = the service id. For an `ai-translate` plugin the job reads, once when it starts, the stored config (`controller.storedConfig`) and the song's note (`lyric_translation_prefs.instruction`), saves the snapshot `settings_json` with the result (`translation_ai_tune.dart`: `encodeAiSnapshot`, `{model, style, instructionHash}` of the values a run reads, as `pluginEffectiveValue`; a failed read saves NULL and still translates) and passes the note to every chunk as `configAppend`: after the required check, the script's `customInstruction` is the shared text, `\n`, the note (`combinedInstruction`, either left out when empty, held to 4001 code points; the field's own `required`, `maxLength` apply to the shared stored value only). `CachedTranslation` and `TranslationReady` carry `settingsJson`, and `TranslationJobStatus.saved` is the `translationFingerprint` of the saved lines and snapshot, so a retranslation with the same lines under other settings still replaces the version on screen. `aiTuneChanges` compares a snapshot with the current values (model, style, instruction hash; keys a snapshot lacks are skipped; a model or style no longer among the options is `removedOption`). Limits: 1000 unique lines and 20 000 characters (`tooLong`). A plugin gets the title and artist of the playing track (a title that is only the file name is never sent) and, once per job, all the unique lines as context. `PluginTranslator` asks the live settings before every chunk and send (translation on, notice accepted, still the effective service). `cancel(key)` stops a forced job (Retranslate) only: every forced run holds its own token, `cancel` removes the status at once (the older version shows again), unsent chunks are dropped (`TranslationSuperseded`) and the chunk in flight is discarded without saving or a `failed` status (a cancelled run drops its partial chunks, a newer run's are kept); a run whose token is gone never touches the status again. `run` returns the status it ended with (`saved`, `failed`) or null. A chunk that reaches the front of the process-wide gate for a job that is no longer the one on screen (`setCurrent`) and is not forced is dropped unsent (`TranslationSuperseded`): the job ends without a failure and keeps its chunks. Lines whose translation is empty or equals the source are dropped; no usable line and a song not in the target language is `emptyResult` and nothing is saved. A song whose dominant detected language is the target is saved as such and shown as a note. A saved row with no lines and another source language reads as missing.
- **Failures** are remembered per key (hash, target, service) until Retry, Retranslate or "Keep the old version" (`dismissRetranslateFailure`); no provider retries (`retry: (_, _) => null`) and no other automatic resend. An unexpected error logs only its type, never the message.
- **Controller actions**: `retranslate()` forces the selected service and replaces only its row, keeps the version on screen meanwhile and returns how the job ended (`saved`, `failed`, or null); `cancelRetranslate()` cancels it; `dismissRetranslateFailure()` hides a failed retranslate; the banner also shows for `Ready(retranslateFailure)` ("Could not retranslate; the old version stays", Retry = `retranslate()`, "Keep the old version"); `retry()`; `setSongTarget(code)`. The segment is "This song" on every open (the last choice is not remembered); it is locked (dimmed, tooltip with the reason) while translated lyrics are off or no song with lyrics is playing, and "All songs" is preselected then; "Queue" is locked while translated lyrics are off or the queue is empty, and is open when no song has lyrics. "Queue" has no caption, since the songs' own choices are not counted per song in the queue. N counts the song-choice rows whose plugin is installed and whose stamp is newer than `allChosenAt` (the ones that can still win against the default, the playing song included), read from the loaded providers. Picking a service first asks the network notice in a dialog above the picker (the picker's `confirm:`: Cancel keeps the picker open and writes nothing); Continue closes the picker and calls `applyServicePick` (`translation_service_pick.dart`) on the parent modal's context without calling `retranslate()`, then the clock is read; "This song" accepts the notice and writes the song's row with `chosen_at`, leaving the default and `allChosenAt` as they are; "Queue" accepts the notice and calls `queueTranslationChoiceProvider.notifier.set(service, chosenAt: clock())`, leaving the default as it is; "All songs" changes the default service and stamps `allChosenAt` (`enableTranslation` when there was no default or the notice is still needed while shown, else `setTranslation`). For "This song" without a song hash or database nothing is asked or written. A failed song write shows the snackbar "Couldn't save the choice for this song" and keeps the notice acceptance. No choice is ever deleted, so the newest explicit choice wins. The service row's subtitle says which choice decided: "Only this song · default: <name>" ("Only this song" alone when no default is installed) or "For the current queue (N songs)" (N = `queue.order.length`) or "Default service for every song". A service of kind `ai-translate` carries an "AI" chip (`TranslationAiBadge`; the service picker passes it through `SelectionRow.badge`) and, when it has a tune (`translationAiTunesProvider`, sync, from the live records: the three standard fields) and the section is shown, a second row "AI tuning" with the title of the style a run would use (`aiOptionLabel`) that opens `openAiTuneModal`. That child modal (`translation_ai_tune_modal.dart`) holds only the model and the style (`AiTuneSelectField`: a tonal box that opens a `SelectionPicker`, saved at once to the plugin config with `commitForgetsFailures` false, so a failed song is not sent again and a running job keeps its chunks; the values come live from `aiTuneValuesProvider`, which holds only `model`, `style`, `customInstruction` and `model.custom`, never other keys) and the "Note for this song" box (`TranslationSongInstructionField`, written to `lyric_translation_prefs.instruction` under the lyrics hash captured when the modal opened, never the hash playing now; a note or pick left unsaved when the stack closes, even because the track changed, is saved on dispose and nothing is retranslated). The shared instruction and every other field are edited in Settings only. A `custom` model with no entry shows "No models yet" and a button to the plugin's settings page; models are added and removed in Settings only. Below the service section, `TranslationStatusLine` renders only when needed: `TranslationLoading` ("Translating…" + Cancel → `toggleLyricsTranslation`), `TranslationReady(refreshing: true)` or `TranslationSameLanguage(refreshing: true)` ("Retranslating…" + Cancel → `cancelRetranslate`), `TranslationFailed`, `TranslationReady(retranslateFailure)` or `TranslationSameLanguage(retranslateFailure)` (an `errorContainer` card naming the failing service—`choices.selected` for `retranslateFailure`—and `translationFailureMessage`, with `failureOpensPluginSettings` and `failureCanRetry` from `translation_failure_actions.dart`, plus "Keep the old version" → `dismissRetranslateFailure` on `retranslateFailure`), or `TranslationSameLanguage` ("Lyrics are already in <language>", with " (per <service>)" when `fromOtherService`). When `TranslationReady` or `TranslationSameLanguage` has (`fromOtherService: true, refreshing: false, retranslateFailure: null)` and `choices.selected != null`, `LayeredModalBody.footer` holds `TranslationActionBar` ("Showing the version from <saved>" and a `FilledButton` "Translate with <selected>" that triggers `startBackgroundRetranslate`). `retranslateInBackground` (`translation_background_retranslate.dart`) calls `controller.retranslate()` first, then pops the modal (`navigator.pop()`), then awaits the job, showing `messenger.showSnackBar(appSnackBar(kind: success, …))` ("Retranslated with <service>") on the opener's `ScaffoldMessengerState` (captured by `showLyricsTranslationModal`; no snackbar when none) only when the job finishes with `TranslationJobStatus.saved`. When `lyricsTranslationProvider(appLang)` becomes `TranslationUnavailable`, `LyricsTranslationModal` calls `closeTranslationModalStack` in a post-frame callback (`popUntil` the parent modal route and then `pop`), closing any open child picker or notice dialog together with the parent modal while leaving Now Playing open. Retranslate prompts for an `ai-translate` service (`translation_ai_tune_changes.dart`): `watchAiTuneChanges` compares the settings now (saved model and style, shared note, and the note of the song the modal opened on) with the snapshot of the version on screen (`aiTuneChangesOf`: only the selected plugin's own finished version, not `fromOtherService`, not `refreshing`); a difference shows the bottom bar "Differs from the version shown: <old → new · Changed the note>" with Retranslate, and the preview card reads "Translated by <service> · <style of the snapshot>". The tune modal footer is Done when nothing differs and no editor holds an unsaved edit, else Later + Retranslate; Retranslate locks the buttons, runs `flushAll()` (3 s limit), pops once with `true` only when everything saved, and the parent then calls `startBackgroundRetranslate`. Changing a setting in the modal never sends a request by itself.
- **`T` key and secondary tap** (`now_playing_screen.dart`, `now_playing_translation_toggle.dart`, `lyrics_translation_button.dart`, same guards as `V` and `L`, both layouts): plain `T`, `KeyDownEvent`, no modifier, no text field focused, no open popup or modal (`!_floatingOpen`). `quickToggleLyricsTranslation` turns off with `toggleLyricsTranslation`; turning on needs lyrics and enables the **default** service only (it never changes the default and never stamps `allChosenAt`); when the song resolves to another service whose notice is not accepted yet it asks that notice too, and cancelling that still turns translation on with the default. With a default selected it does that; with none and the plugin records ready it calls `openModal` (it never shows a snackbar). Both `T` (`openModal: () => _translationModalOpener.value?.call()`) and right-click on `LyricsTranslationButton` (`openModal: _openModal`) open `LyricsTranslationModal` when no service is selected. Inside the modal, the translation switch passes `openModal: openServicePicker`, so turning on without a chosen service opens the service child picker directly. The network notice (`lib/shared/translation_notice_dialog.dart`) opens when the service is not in `noticeAcknowledged`: Continue writes `enableTranslation(service)` (select, accept, show, one write), Cancel changes nothing. The modal switch, the service picker, and the settings screen picker reuse the same helpers.
- **Links to Settings**: the translation modal header has a `⚙` icon button ("Translation settings", `openTranslationSettings` in `translation_modal_navigation.dart`) that closes the modal stack and Now Playing and opens `/settings/playback?focus=translation` (see `SettingsAnchor` in `lib/shared/settings_routes.dart`); the Playback page scrolls to the target and pulses it. A failed translation shows a banner (`lyrics_translation_banner.dart`) in the lyrics pane and an error card in the modal (`TranslationStatusLine`): a translate plugin's key error (missing, rejected or locked key) has "Open settings", which closes Now Playing (and the modal stack when triggered from the modal) and opens the plugin's page (`translationPluginRoute(id)`); quota and rate limit keep Retry. The automatic online-lyrics notice shown from Now Playing has an "Open lyrics settings" link that does the same for the Lyrics group (`?focus=lyrics`); it counts as declining, so nothing is turned on.
- **Service names** (`translation_service_names.dart`, `shared/translation_service_name.dart`): `translationPluginInfosProvider` maps install ids to name, hosts and removed state, from live and removed translate records. A plugin shows its name, and "<name> (removed)" once uninstalled (a kept translation still shows its name). The notice dialog says it is a plugin and always lists the hosts, with "Kanaru does not control how the service uses them". The modal, the tooltip and the settings picker use these names (the header pill shows the plugin's icon and the target language name).
- **Settings rows** (Settings > Playback > Translation, `features/settings/lyrics_translation_setting_rows.dart`, `translation_plugin_rows.dart`): the service picker (`translationServiceChoicesProvider`, shared with the modal) lists the live translate plugins (always a dropdown; picking from `null`, or switching while translation is on to a service without an accepted notice, goes through `enableTranslationWithNotice` and Cancel leaves the settings unchanged; a stored plugin that is gone resolves to `null` and shows "Not selected"; while no plugin is installed or while the plugin records load or fail it is disabled), one row per installed translate plugin that opens its page, a "Get translation services" row that opens the store on the Translate chip, the default language (`targetLanguage`, null follows the app) and, in Settings > Data & storage > Caches (`translation_cache_setting_row.dart`), the saved translations (`translationCacheStatsProvider`, clear through `confirmDestructive`; the per-song language picks stay). Picking the default here never stamps `allChosenAt`, so a song's own choice keeps winning.
- **Privacy**: lyric lines leave the machine only to the selected service, only after its notice was accepted, and only while a Now Playing widget shows the song. A translate plugin's script also gets the song's title, artist and lyrics text (`song` in `translate.js`) and may send them to the plugin's declared hosts; the notice lists those hosts (see [lyrics-plugin-api.md](./lyrics-plugin-api.md#data-and-consent)). Tests replace the plugin runner and never reach the network or the keyring.

#### Outgoing requests

Every request the lyrics features can make. The app has no telemetry and sends nothing else for them.

| Host | Used by | Sent | Only when |
|---|---|---|---|
| The hosts a translate plugin declares | Lyrics translation (translate plugin) | Lyric lines, target language and song title, artist and lyrics text, as the plugin's script puts them in its requests | The plugin is the chosen service, its notice accepted, a Now Playing widget shows the song |

File paths, file names and device information are never sent. The `.lrc` writer and the cache do not use the network.
